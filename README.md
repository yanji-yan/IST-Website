# 🎓 IST Batch Website

Welcome to the official website of our **IST Batch**.

This website is created exclusively for our batch. It serves as a digital space where we can keep track of our **students, achievements, events, awards, memories, and important happenings throughout our journey together.**

---

## 🌐 About the Website

Our batch website is a place where everyone can see and remember what happens throughout our school year.

It contains information about our students and documents the moments that make our batch special — from everyday activities to major achievements and awards.

### 📌 What You Can Find

* **Student Information** — Profiles and information about our batch members
* **Achievements** — Academic, extracurricular, and personal achievements
* **Awards** — Awards and recognitions received by our students
* **Daily Events** — Important activities and events happening in our batch
* **Batch Updates** — Things happening within our class and school activities
* **Memories** — Photos and memorable moments from our journey
* **Celebrations** — Special occasions and batch activities

---

## 🎯 Purpose

The main purpose of this website is to **document and preserve our batch journey**.
